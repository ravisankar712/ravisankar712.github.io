Over the years, I have designed and taught classes on advanced topics in Quantum Field Theory (unofficially) to my classmates and colleagues. Some of these lectures are recorded (thanks to Dima and Nur for the initiative!) and have been put on YouTube. Listed below are those lectures with corresponding lecture notes.

Lectures on bosonization.

In $1+1$ dimensions, fermions and bosons have little distinction. It turns out that one can write a theory of fermions entirely in terms of a theory of bosons. The particles in one theory is mapped to solitions in the other theory. This is the story of bosonization. These four lectures attempt to elucidate this concept. Taught at UVA during the summer of 2025.

Lectures on phase transitions and the Renormalization Group.

This is an unofficial course taught at UVA during the spring of 2026. These are lectures on critical phenomena and phase transitions using the renormalization group methods.

Lectures on Perturbative Renormalization in Scalar Field Theory.

This is an unofficial class taught at UVA during fall of 2022. These lectures attempts to explain the techniques of perturbative renormalization in the simplest context of a scalar field theory. Starting from path integrals and LSZ formula, we go through the $\phi^4$ theory and discuss the mass and coupling constant renormalization. The first lecture is by my friend Gabriel Sousa.
