Hello world! I am a PhD student (2021 – present) at the University of Virginia, working with professor Diana Vaman.

Broadly speaking, my research is about quantum field theory and its relation with classical and quantum gravity. With my advisor and other collaborators, I have been working on extracting classical observables from quantum scattering, charged black holes and weak gravity conjecture, and aspects of quantum entanglement in conformal field theories and holography. You can read more about it in the reasearch page.

I am also passionate about teaching physics. During my PhD years, I have taught classes on some advanced topics in Quantum Field Theory – namely perturbative renormalization in QFT, bosonization, and phase transitions and critical phenomena. These lectures are on YouTube (thanks to my awesome classmates!). See the teaching page for the videos and lecture notes.

I grew up in an island lying between the Arabian sea and the backwaters of the southern Indian state of Kerala. After schooling in Kerala, I moved to West Bengal to pursue a BS - MS degree in physics. From 2021 fall, I am a PhD candidate at the University of Virginia department of physics.
